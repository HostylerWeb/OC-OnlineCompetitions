Feature checklist

Online Competitions
Website & admin capabilities

A client-ready checklist of what the platform delivers today: customer website, checkout, compliance, optional shop, and staff back office.

Online Competitions | Hostyler Group
Scope summary. Each checked item is a delivered capability on the Online Competitions platform (customer site, checkout, compliance, optional shop, and admin back office).

Main customer website

Browse & enter competitions

  • Home page with hero slider, categories, “ending soon” highlights, recent winners, and calls to action (sections can be turned on/off in admin).
  • Full competition catalogue with filters by category.
  • Competition detail pages: photos, description, ticket price, prize value, draw date, tickets sold.
  • Choose how many tickets to buy; optional per-person limit per competition.
  • Admin chooses per competition whether a skill question is required; customer must answer correctly when enabled.
  • Some prizes can be marked as cash alternatives instead of a physical item.
  • Live progress bar showing how full the competition is.
  • Public entry list (ticket numbers) on the competition and on a dedicated entries page.

Main prize draw

  • Scheduled draw date and time (not brought forward because tickets sell quickly).
  • Winners chosen using a verified random number generator.
  • Draws can be run during a live video stream.
  • Winners emailed, shown on the Winners page; prizes fulfilled after verification (typical dispatch within about 2 days).
  • Prize delivery within the UK.

Instant wins (selected competitions)

  • Extra prizes on top of the main draw, revealed when tickets are purchased.
  • Competition page shows each instant prize, availability, and claimed winning ticket numbers (ticket-style display).
  • Instant prize types:
    • Physical prizes (e.g. luxury goods, gadgets).
    • Cash prizes.
    • Site credit to the customer wallet.
    • Free tickets to another competition (can be multiple per win).
  • Celebration screen after purchase and email confirmation for instant wins.

Milestones & bonus prizes

  • Bonus prizes unlock at set milestones (e.g. 25%, 50%, 75% of tickets sold).
  • Milestones shown on the competition page (unlocked vs still to come).
  • When a milestone is reached, eligible ticket holders can win the bonus prize (separate from main draw and instant wins).
  • Bonus prizes: physical items or free tickets to another competition.
  • Bonus wins recorded on the account and fulfilled by the team after confirmation.

Shopping basket & checkout

  • Cart with tickets from one or more competitions.
  • Promo codes for discounts or bonus tickets (one code per order; expiry dates).
  • Apply referral reward tickets at checkout.
  • Pay with site credit from the wallet (full or partial).
  • UK addresses only at checkout (United Kingdom).
  • Integrated payment gateways (admin enables which are live):
    • Stripe: cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay where supported.
    • Paytriot: hosted payment page or on-site popup checkout (3D Secure where required).
  • Card details handled by the payment provider; the site does not store card numbers.
  • Automatic ticket issue on successful payment; confirmation email and dashboard visibility.

AI personalisation (customer-facing)

  • OpenRouter-backed personalisation (model chosen in admin).
  • Server can trigger on-brand prompts from browsing, basket, orders, and wins (e.g. tailored upsell popup).
  • Rules and copy controlled in admin; customers see approved prompts only.

Customer accounts

  • Email and password with verification.
  • One-time code or magic link sign-in.
  • Google sign-in; guest browsing merges when the customer registers.
  • Dashboard: overview, tickets, orders, wins, wallet, referrals, profile, responsible play.
  • Short referral links (e.g. /r/yourcode).

Refer a friend

  • Personal referral link and code from the dashboard.
  • Rewards when friends join and make a qualifying purchase (typically free tickets).
  • Tiered rewards for more active referrals.
  • Referral leaderboard.
  • Referral ticket wallet applied at checkout (per admin referral settings).

Wallet & site credit

  • Wallet balance (top-ups, refunds, admin adjustments, some instant-win prizes).
  • Transaction history in the dashboard.
  • Site credit reduces or covers ticket purchases at checkout.

Free postal entry

  • No-purchase postal route for active competitions (address on Free Postal Entry page).
  • Postal entries treated equally in the draw alongside paid entries.

Responsible play & compliance

  • 18+ only; age checked at registration and checkout.
  • Optional personal monthly spend limit (lower limits immediate; higher after 24-hour cooling-off).
  • Self-exclusion: 6 months, 1 year, 5 years, or permanent.
  • Links to support organisations (e.g. GamCare).

Content & trust

  • How it works, About, FAQ (competitions, instant wins, milestones, referrals, payments, delivery).
  • Terms, privacy, cookies, responsible play, contact.
  • Winners showcase on home and full winners listing.
  • Contact form for enquiries.

Mobile

  • Dedicated mobile app experience (shared account, competitions, checkout, dashboard).

Online shop (optional separate storefront)

Already built; can be activated when required.

  • Product catalogue with categories and product detail pages.
  • Variants (e.g. size/colour) where configured.
  • Cart, checkout, order history, and customer account.
  • Shipping, refunds, terms, privacy, contact pages.

Admin / back office

Overview

  • Dashboard with sales snapshots and quick links.
  • Staff accounts; sensitive settings restricted to full administrators.

Competitions & catalogue

  • Full competition lifecycle: draft, live, paused, ended, pending draw, drawn, cancelled.
  • Pricing, prize value, ticket pool, optional per-user cap, end and draw dates.
  • Featured and hero slider placement; categories and display order.
  • Rich content: descriptions, images, hero, social preview image, optional video.
  • Skill question per competition (optional).
  • Cash prize marking; instant wins and milestone bonuses per competition.

Instant prizes (admin)

  • Template library (title, description, value, images).
  • Types: physical, cash, site credit, free tickets to another competition.
  • Link to competitions, quantities, winning ticket numbers, ordering, archive.
  • Instant win fulfilment queue (claimed / unclaimed).

Milestones & bonus awards (admin)

  • Bonus prize templates and per-competition milestone percentages.
  • Automatic bonus draw when milestones hit; staff fulfilment tracking.

Orders & promotions

  • Competition orders: items, payment status, ticket numbers.
  • Promo codes (percentage, fixed, bonus tickets, limits, expiry).
  • Shop products, variants, categories, and orders when shop is enabled.

Customers & referrals

  • Customer search, profiles, orders, tickets, wins, referral activity.
  • Referral programme configuration (qualifying rules, tiers, wallet distribution).
  • Referral statistics and network map.

Fulfilment

  • Main draw winners: ticket, contact, fulfilment status.
  • Instant and bonus win queues with bulk actions and restore.
  • Manual postal entry processing.

Media & content

  • Media library for uploads and reuse.
  • Homepage layout editor (section order and on/off).
  • SEO and social preview defaults.

Live draws

  • Livestream draws list and full-screen Draw Studio.
  • Optional Google Sheets logging for draw results.

Payments (admin)

  • Stripe: sandbox/live, enable/disable, default method.
  • Paytriot: sandbox/live, hosted vs popup checkout.
  • Credential testing; site credit payments; Stripe webhook registration.

Compliance & safer play

  • Compliance settings: age rules, instant-win credit card rules, postal entry address and prominence.
  • Safer-play activity on customer accounts for support.

Communications

  • Email delivery settings (orders, wins, verification, magic links).
  • Push notification campaigns and device subscriptions.

Marketing & technical extras

  • Conversion and affiliate tracking (pixels, partner campaigns).
  • Addons (e.g. media converter for image/video optimisation).

AI integration (admin – OpenRouter)

  • Save OpenRouter API key and enable/disable AI personalisation.
  • Scan free or paid models live from OpenRouter; pick model and tone.
  • Configure allowed customer actions (upsell popup, competition highlight, ticket bundles, spend limits).
  • Brand instructions and guardrails; server-side decisions before the storefront shows a prompt.

Security

  • Admin access separate from customer logins; payment keys and secrets stay in the back office.
